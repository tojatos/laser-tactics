Laser Tactics is a chess-like strategy game designed for two players.

gps_fixed The goal is to strategically manipulate a Laser-firing piece and various reflective objects to eliminate your opponent's King.

Taking a Turn

Available Actions

A turn consists of two actions. Players can choose from the following:

  • Move a piece
  • Rotate a piece
  • Shoot the Laser Beam (only once per turn)

Movement Rules

Moving a piece by one square consumes one action. Moving two squares takes two actions, although a piece can move two squares in a single step.

Pieces can move forward, backward, left, or right, but not diagonally. To effectively move diagonally, a player must use two moves (e.g., forward and then right).

Restrictions

A piece cannot be moved onto an occupied square, except in the following cases:

  • Block and King captures: These pieces can capture by moving onto an enemy piece.
  • Hypercube moves: The Hypercube can teleport itself and another piece.

Rotating a Piece

When a piece is selected for rotation, it turns 90 degrees in the chosen direction. Players may continue rotating a piece until they deselect it.

Rotating a piece to face any direction consumes one action. The action is deducted only after the piece is deselected.

Laser Tactics Strategy

Strategic Placement

Like in chess, much of Laser Tactics strategy revolves around thoughtful placement of your pieces. However, the game differs significantly from chess in key ways:

  • The Laser can strike across long distances and in multiple directions at once.
  • The Hypercube introduces an element of uncertainty by teleporting pieces.

The best strategy depends on your opponent's skill and playstyle, but the following general tips may help.

Effective Use of Mirrors

Deploy mirrors early to maximize the potential of your Laser. Positioning a network of mirrors on both sides of a Beam Splitter allows you to deal as much damage as possible.

Controlling the Board with Blocks

Blocks control surrounding areas with the threat of capture, restricting your opponent's movement. To make the most of them:

  • Rotate the reflective side of a Block to the most probable Laser attack direction.
  • Forcing your opponent to destroy a Block often requires them to use multiple pieces.

Defending the King

Surrounding your King with Straight and Diagonal Mirrors makes it immune to Laser attacks. In this case, your opponent must rely on Blocks to break your defense.

Advanced strategy: If your opponent runs out of Blocks, your King becomes nearly invulnerable. Defending your King with Blocks can also be effective.

Using the Hypercube Wisely

The Hypercube should be used cautiously, as you cannot predict where a relocated piece will reappear.

Most players reserve the Hypercube as a last resort—if a piece is about to be destroyed, teleporting it might be worth the risk. Another advanced technique is:

  • If your opponent's King is surrounded by Mirrors, use your Hypercube and Block to break their defense.
  • Escorting the Hypercube with a Block prevents counterattacks.
  • This strategy may force the opponent to either evacuate the King or lose it.

Summary

  • Position mirrors early to maximize your Laser's effectiveness.
  • Use Blocks to control areas and limit your opponent's mobility.
  • Protect your King using a combination of Mirrors and Blocks.
  • Use the Hypercube strategically—it can either be an advantage or a liability.
