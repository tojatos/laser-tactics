Rules

Laser Tactics is a chesslike strategy game for two players.

The goal is to manipulate a Laser-firing piece and various reflective objects to eliminate your opponent's King.

Laser Tactics Strategy

Some general strategy tips:

  • Position mirrors early to maximize your Laser’s effectiveness.
  • Use Blocks to control areas and make movement difficult for your opponent.
  • Protect your King with Mirrors or Blocks.
  • Use the Hypercube strategically—it can either help or hinder your game.
